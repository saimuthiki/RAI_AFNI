# FDE pitch: show what you built and the outcome it delivers

Inputs: everything under `hackathon/work/`.

1. **Impact model** (`hackathon/work/05-impact-model.md`): use `financial-analyst` / `cfo-advisor`
   and `market-sizing-analysis` to build conservative, base and stretch cases. Show the formula
   for every number (volume × time saved × loaded cost, revenue recovered, risk avoided), and cite
   discovery quotes or `[EST]` assumptions. Add the Evoke-wide scale-out view (other accounts,
   business units, external clients as a sellable offering).
2. **Story** (`data-storytelling`, `executive-mentor`, `board-deck-builder`), in 30 minutes:
   - 3 min hook: the stakeholder's pain in their own words, and what it costs.
   - 2 min "we challenged the brief": the reframe.
   - 12 min live demo of the outcome, not the features. The before/after is visible on screen.
   - 5 min "how 3 humans + N agents built it": pod chart, pod-log stats, discovery artifacts, test evidence.
   - 3 min impact, rollout plan and the next 90 days.
   - 5 min Q&A.
3. **Deck**: use `pptx` (PowerPoint) or `frontend-slides` (HTML), plus `architecture-diagram-creator`
   and `timeline-creator` for visuals. One idea per slide; numbers big.
4. **Demo script** (`hackathon/work/05-demo-script.md`): click-by-click, who speaks, and the fallback
   if something breaks (backup video timestamp).
5. **Q&A war room**: use `scenario-war-room` + `roast` to list the 15 hardest questions a CEO, CFO,
   CTO or delivery head would ask (cost to run, data security, adoption, why not buy, accuracy,
   who maintains it), with crisp answers.
