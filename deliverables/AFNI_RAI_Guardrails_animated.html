MOST GUARDRAIL STACKS

  • A paid LLM-judge on every check — no cheap tier
  • Fail-OPEN defaults — an erroring rail lets traffic through
  • Silent check-dropping — try/except returns “clean”
  • No published precision/recall — a README claim

AFNI RAI

  • Cost-ordered cascade — free on 100%, paid on the surviving slice
  • Fail-CLOSED + fail-LOUD, enforced once in the engine
  • Every rail cites the source file:line it was ported from
  • Full attribution: rail, confidence kind, entity, fingerprint (never the secret)
  • Vendor-neutral: OpenAI · Gemini · local behind one contract
